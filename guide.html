    How it works

    Three steps from “giveaway!” to winner

    💬

    Set a timer with 3-2-1 beeps. When it hits zero, the roulette spins on its own.

    A winner is revealed

    Confetti, the prize and its image pop up — and the bot can @ the winner in chat.

    Setup guide

    Live on your stream in 2 minutes

    Two browser sources in OBS. No downloads, no accounts.

    1

    Add it to OBS

    Two pieces from the same page — the wheel goes on your stream, the controls stay private in a dock.

    On stream https://abdulazizjhk.github.io/giveaway-wheel/overlay.html
    Your controls https://abdulazizjhk.github.io/giveaway-wheel/dock.html
    1. In your scene, Sources → ➕ Browser → paste the On stream URL → set 1920 × 1080 → uncheck “Shutdown source when not visible”. It’s transparent, so it sits over your scene.
    2. Top menu Docks → Custom Browser Docks… → name it “Giveaway” → paste the Your controls URL → Apply. Dock it wherever you like — only you see this.
    ⟳
    Set these two links once — they never change. Each time OBS opens them they load the newest version, and a running dock or source updates itself between giveaways. Keep them apart: the On stream link is see-through and belongs in a Browser Source, not a dock.
    2

    Run a giveaway

    1. In the dock’s 🎡 Giveaway tab, type your Twitch channel → Connect (no login needed — it reads chat anonymously).
    2. Set a Timer (e.g. 60s) and click ▶ Open Entries in the bar at the bottom of the dock. Viewers type !join to enter — the on-stream banner shows the live count + countdown.
    3. When the timer ends it auto-spins. Or press Spin / Space any time.
    4. The winner is revealed with confetti — and if you added rewards, the prize (and its image) shows too.
    5. Tip: turn on Chat control in the dock’s 💬 Chat bot tab and you or your mods can type !gw open, !gw close or !gw spin right in chat.
    3

    Add rewards & prizes

    • Add prizes in the dock’s 🎁 Rewards tab. In Entrants mode the winner automatically gets the top reward; in Rewards mode the roulette picks the prize and gives it to a random entrant.
    • Click the 🖼️ next to a reward to attach an image (logo / prize art) — it reveals big when that prize is won.
    4

    Chat announcements OPTIONAL

    Want the bot to post “🏆 @winner won {reward}!” to chat? In the dock’s 💬 Chat bot tab:

    1. Get an OAuth token at twitchtokengenerator.com (scopes: chat:edit, chat:read).
    2. Enter your channel/bot username + the token → Connect bot → tick Post event messages. The token stays only in your browser.
    5

    Make it yours OPTIONAL

    In the dock’s ⚙️ Settings tab — everything updates on stream instantly:

    • Language — English or العربية; Arabic switches the dock and on-stream overlay to right-to-left.
    • Overlay size — Small, Medium or Large, to suit your layout.
    • Theme colour — pick a preset or any custom colour to match your channel.
    • Twitch colours & badges — cells use each viewer’s chat colour and show MOD, VIP and SUB tags.
    • Profile pictures — each viewer’s Twitch picture on their card and the winner screen (an initial if they have none).
    • Winner screen — close it automatically after 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
    • Sound — volume slider for the beeps, ticks and win chime.
    ⟳
    Updates install themselves — the dock and the on-stream source reload onto a new version between giveaways. Winner didn’t claim it? The 🎡 Giveaway tab lists the last 5 winners — Re-roll puts the reward back and spins again.
